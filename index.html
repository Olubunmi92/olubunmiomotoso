FEATURED CASE STUDY

SmartLib Digital Library Management System

Role: Project ManagerMethod: Hybrid Waterfall + Agile ScrumDuration: 9 months

The challenge

The legacy library system primarily supported basic cataloguing. Members often needed to visit branches physically to check availability, renew loans or resolve fines, while staff relied on manual processes and lacked real-time cross-branch visibility.

The response

A hybrid delivery approach was structured around stable infrastructure and governance alongside iterative development of user-facing web, mobile and self-service capabilities.

01RequirementsUnderstand users, branches and constraints
02DesignDefine architecture, journeys and priorities
03DevelopmentBuild web, mobile and kiosk capabilities
04MigrationDigitise and transition legacy information
05TestingValidate functionality and performance
06DeploymentTrain teams and transition into use

Delivery objectives

  • Increase member satisfaction from 62% to 85%.
  • Reduce average book search time from 15 minutes to 2 minutes.
  • Enable at least 70% of transactions through self-service.
  • Provide real-time inventory visibility across 15 branches.
  • Introduce analytics for procurement and resource allocation.
  • Target 99.5% uptime during defined operating hours.

Methodology & tools

Waterfall was applied to stable infrastructure, governance, migration and deployment planning, while Agile Scrum supported evolving user-facing features through short iterations, feedback and continuous testing.

JiraConfluenceMicrosoft TeamsMicrosoft ProjectTrelloScrum
Project principle: Technology is only useful when it improves the experience of the people and teams who rely on it.
